Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

{{APP_NAME}} ("we", "us") is a fitness app for training, cardio, nutrition and community. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. It applies to the {{APP_NAME}} Android and iOS apps and the website at {{WEB_URL}}.

The data fiduciary (controller) is {{LEGAL_OWNER}}, operating {{APP_NAME}}. Contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

1. Data we collect

You give us

  • Account: name, email address, username, profile photo, and the sign-in method you use (Google, Apple or email).
  • Profile and body data: age, gender, height, weight, body measurements, fitness goals and experience level.
  • Training data: workouts, sets, reps, weights, plans, skills, personal records and notes.
  • Cardio data: runs, walks and rides with time, distance, pace, elevation and the GPS route, recorded only while you are tracking an activity.
  • Nutrition data: meals, foods, portions, water, food photos you take or choose, barcodes you scan, and nutrition goals.
  • AI coach conversations: the messages you send to the AI coach and the replies.
  • Community content: posts, comments, likes, clans, events, challenges, chat messages and reports you make.
  • Marketplace: listings you publish and, if you ask to list, the business and contact details you send us.
  • Support: anything you send when you contact us.

Collected automatically

  • Location: precise location only while you track a cardio activity or use a location feature, with your permission. We do not track your location in the background when you are not recording an activity.
  • Physical activity: step counts from your phone's activity sensors, with your permission.
  • Device and app: a push-notification token, device platform, app version and basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service.
  • Purchases: if you buy {{APP_NAME}} Pro on our website, our payment partner Razorpay processes your payment. We receive the subscription or order ID, status and amount, never your card or UPI details.

We do not use advertising SDKs, we do not show third-party ads, and we do not sell your personal data.

2. How we use your data

  • To run the app: your account, training logs, analytics, plans, nutrition tracking and history.
  • To calculate things for you, such as calories, training load, step totals, records and your athlete rank.
  • To power AI features you choose to use (AI coach, food photo analysis, workout plans and summaries).
  • To run the community: showing your profile and content to others according to your privacy settings, and moderating abuse.
  • To send notifications you turn on (reminders, social activity, clan and event updates).
  • To manage your Pro subscription and prevent fraud.
  • To keep the service secure and to comply with the law.

We process your data with your consent (given when you sign up and grant permissions) and to provide the service you asked for. You can withdraw consent at any time by changing permissions, editing your privacy settings or deleting your account.

3. AI processing

When you use an AI feature, the relevant data (for example your message, a food photo, or a summary of your recent training) is sent to an AI model provider to generate the answer. Our providers are Google (Gemini), Groq, NVIDIA and OpenRouter. They process the data to return a response under their own terms. We do not send your name or email to them. AI answers can be wrong and are not medical advice.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other users: your username, display name, photo and the posts, activities and stats you choose to share, according to your profile and activity privacy settings. Content in clans and events is visible to their members.
  • Service providers who process data for us:
    • Google Firebase: sign-in, database, notifications and website hosting.
    • Render and Supabase: our backend servers and database.
    • Razorpay: payments on the website.
    • The AI providers listed in section 3.
    • OpenStreetMap and Esri: map tiles for the area you view.
    • Open-Elevation: route coordinates, to calculate elevation.
    • Open Food Facts: barcodes you scan.
    • YouTube: exercise videos, embedded in privacy-enhanced mode.
    • UI Avatars: your display name's initials, for default profile pictures.
    • OtaKit: delivering app updates.
  • Shops you visit: gear and marketplace links open the seller's or store's own site (such as Amazon). What you do there is covered by their privacy policy. We may earn a commission from some links.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or to protect users and the service.

Our providers may store data on servers outside India. We use providers with appropriate security and contractual safeguards.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your profile, training, nutrition and community data within 30 days; copies in backups are removed on their normal cycle. We keep payment and subscription records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Settings → Your data → Export.
  • Correct: edit your profile and entries at any time.
  • Control visibility: Settings → Privacy.
  • Permissions: turn location, activity, camera and notifications on or off in your phone settings.
  • Delete: Settings → Danger zone → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Grievances: write to our grievance contact, {{LEGAL_OWNER}}, at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children

{{APP_NAME}} is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by security rules so users can only reach data they are allowed to see, and payment details never touch our servers. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device safe.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
