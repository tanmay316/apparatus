How to delete your account and data

Delete it in the app

  1. Open {{APP_NAME}} and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (menu → Settings).
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Can't open the app?

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my {{APP_NAME}} account". Include your username if you know it. We verify that the request comes from the account owner and delete the account within 30 days, then confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, photo, username and sign-in account.
  • Workouts, plans, cardio activities and routes, steps, body measurements, skills and records.
  • Nutrition logs, food photos and AI coach conversations.
  • Your posts, comments, likes, clan memberships, event and challenge entries, and marketplace listings.

What we keep

  • Payment and subscription records, for as long as tax and accounting law requires (no card or UPI details are ever stored by us).
  • Clans, events or challenges you created that other people still use are transferred to the {{APP_NAME}} team so their members don't lose them.
  • Backup copies are removed on their normal cycle, within 30 days.

If you have a Pro subscription, deleting your account cancels future renewals on Google Play. You can also cancel it yourself first in Settings → {{APP_NAME}} Pro → Manage plan, or in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.
