# Privacy Policy for Apparatus / Swasth

**Effective date:** October 10, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Apparatus / Swasth ("we," "our," or "the app") is a fitness and wellness application designed to help users manage workouts, physical activity, fitness progress, nutrition, and related features.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our application.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following information:

- **Account information:** Information such as your name, email address, and authentication details when you create or access an account.
- **Fitness and workout information:** Workout routines, exercises, sets, repetitions, workout history, fitness goals, and progress records that you choose to enter or track.
- **Physical activity information:** Activity recognition or movement-related data, if the relevant feature is enabled and the required permission is granted.
- **Nutrition information:** Food logs, dietary preferences, and nutrition-related information that you choose to provide.
- **User-generated content:** Information you submit through community or marketplace features, if available.
- **Technical information:** Device and app information, diagnostic logs, and other technical data where collected by the app or its service providers.

We collect only the information necessary for the features you use, subject to the permissions you grant and the applicable settings.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Provide workout planning, fitness tracking, and progress monitoring.
- Support physical activity tracking where available.
- Provide nutrition and wellness features.
- Enable community or marketplace functionality where available.
- Maintain app functionality, security, and reliability.
- Troubleshoot technical issues and improve the application.
- Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Permissions and Health-Related Data

The app may request access to physical activity recognition or other health-related permissions to provide specific fitness features.

We use activity-related information only for the relevant functionality described in the app. Access to device health or activity data depends on the features implemented and the permissions granted by the user.

You can manage applicable permissions through your device settings. Revoking a permission may affect features that depend on it.

## 5. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to provide application functionality, authentication, data storage, and technical support.

For example, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore may be used to authenticate users and store application data, where applicable to the deployed version of the app.

These providers may process information on our behalf in accordance with their applicable terms and privacy documentation. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of relevant service providers.

We do not authorize service providers to use personal information for purposes unrelated to providing their services, subject to the applicable service terms.

## 6. Sharing of Information

We may share information with:

- **Service providers:** Providers that support authentication, hosting, storage, security, diagnostics, or other app functionality.
- **Other users:** Information you choose to make visible through community or marketplace features, if those features are available.
- **Legal authorities:** Where disclosure is required by applicable law or is necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

We do not sell personal information to third parties. This statement applies to our own practices and does not replace any separate disclosures required for third-party SDKs or services.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the app's functionality, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

Where account creation is supported, users should be provided with an appropriate way to request account deletion, including any applicable Google Play account-deletion requirements.

## 8. Data Security

## 9. Children's Privacy
