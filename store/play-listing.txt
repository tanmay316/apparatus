SENSITIVE PERMISSIONS / DECLARATIONS
------------------------------------
Foreground service types (Policy > App content > Foreground service permissions):
• FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION: records the GPS route, distance and pace of a run, walk or ride the user
  starts. Notification shown the whole time; stops when the user finishes. Record a short screen video
  of starting a run and the notification, upload it (YouTube unlisted) and paste the link.
• FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH: keeps the workout timer and step count running during a workout the user starts.
Location: foreground only (no background location permission).
Physical activity (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION): step counting.
Camera: food photo and barcode scanning, profile photo.


RELEASE CHECKLIST
-----------------
1. powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File android\create-upload-key.ps1   (once; back up the .jks + password)
2. Add the printed SHA-1/SHA-256 to Firebase > Project settings > Android app (Google sign-in needs it).
3. build-apk.bat  ->  Apparatus.aab
4. Play Console > Create app > Internal testing > upload the .aab
5. Play Console > Setup > App signing: copy the "App signing key" SHA-1/SHA-256 into Firebase too.
6. Fill everything above, then promote Internal -> Closed testing (new personal developer accounts need
   12+ testers for 14 days) -> Production.
